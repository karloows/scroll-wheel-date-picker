# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1. We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background. We value respectful, constructive dialogue and will not tolerate discrimination, harassment, or abuse in any form.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and inclusive — assume good intent, but address impact.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Listen actively and be open to feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward others.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, unwelcome advances, insults, derogatory language, and any form of abuse — online or offline — are not tolerated.

## Reporting

**For sensitive or confidential matters** (harassment, discrimination, threats, misconduct), contact the maintainer directly:

- **Email:** [github.com/karloows](https://github.com/karloows)

Private reports are handled confidentially and investigated promptly.

**For non-sensitive feedback or general concerns** (e.g., code style discussion, documentation clarity), you can open a GitHub issue.

## Enforcement

Violations of this code of conduct may result in removal from the community, including loss of contribution privileges. Enforcement decisions are made at the discretion of the project maintainer.
